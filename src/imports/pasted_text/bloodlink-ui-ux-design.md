Design a premium, modern, enterprise-grade UI/UX in ravi for an AI-powered Blood Donation & Emergency Donor Management Platform called "BloodLink". The platform solves the problem of the lack of a centralized system connecting blood donors with patients during emergencies, enabling fast donor discovery, AI-based donor matching, real-time emergency requests, hospital coordination, and blood bank inventory management. The design should be pixel-perfect, highly polished, responsive (Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile), and suitable for a real-world healthcare SaaS product.

Use a clean, trustworthy healthcare design language inspired by Apple Human Interface Guidelines, Google Material 3, Stripe, Linear, Notion, Airbnb, and Microsoft Fluent Design. Use Inter or Poppins typography, 8px spacing system, Auto Layout, reusable components, design tokens, accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA), rounded corners (16–20px), soft shadows, subtle gradients, glassmorphism where appropriate, premium healthcare illustrations, smooth micro-interactions, loading skeletons, empty states, hover states, success/error states, and light & dark mode.
